FAQ

Your questions answered

Our dentists' answers on bleeding gums, fillings, implants, root canal treatment and children's teeth.

See all questions

Why do gums bleed, and is it dangerous?

The most common cause is gum inflammation (gingivitis) brought on by plaque and tartar. In the early stage it clears up in 1–2 weeks with a cleaning and good brushing. If neglected, it progresses to bone loss and tooth loss (periodontitis).

Is dental implant treatment painful?

No. It is done under local anaesthesia and you feel no pain during the procedure. There may be mild tenderness for 1–2 days afterwards.

How long do white fillings last?

On average 7–10 years with regular care. The size and position of the filling and any clenching habit affect its lifespan.

At what age should I first take my child to the dentist?

When the first baby tooth appears, and by age 1 at the latest. Meeting the dentist early prevents both decay and fear of the dentist.

Do I need a crown after root canal treatment?

For back teeth, yes; a tooth that has had a root canal is more fragile. For front teeth, a filling is often enough.
