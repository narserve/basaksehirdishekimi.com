Dental Clinic in Başakşehir, Kayaşehir, Istanbul

Ay Yıldız Oral and Dental Health Clinic is a dental clinic in the Seyranşehir Site in Kayaşehir, Başakşehir, Istanbul. We offer dental implants, orthodontics, smile design, root canal treatment, fillings, gum treatment and paediatric dentistry to patients from Kayaşehir, Kayabaşı, Başakşehir, Bahçeşehir, Şahintepe and Altınşehir, as well as international patients visiting Istanbul.
