About Us

At our clinic in Kayaşehir, Istanbul, our aim is to provide high-quality healthcare and a calm, well-equipped environment that frees our patients from the fear of the dentist.

At our clinic we follow the philosophy of “maximum patient comfort with minimum procedures”. Unlike busy clinics that work like a production line, we offer personalised treatment with an eye for aesthetics. Our goal is to be your family's dentist.

We believe that learning should continue throughout life; we follow current knowledge and technology and apply it in our patients' treatments. We provide care in every branch of modern dentistry, particularly aesthetic dentistry, implantology, orthodontics and paediatric dentistry.

The staff who assist our dentists also receive regular training in their fields and keep up with developments. Sterilisation and infection control is the first rule we never compromise on in our clinic.

Our Values

01

An honest treatment plan

We do not recommend procedures you do not need. We explain your options, how long they take and what they cost, openly.

02

Patient comfort

Pain-free, fear-free treatment with digital anaesthesia and a calm environment.

03

Continuous training

Our dentists stay up to date through congresses, courses and certificate programmes.

04

Hygiene

A sterile set and single-use materials for every patient, with regular sterilisation checks.
