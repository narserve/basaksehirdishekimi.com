Dr. Vahide Ceyda Acar

Specialist Paediatric Dentist (Pedodontics)

A paediatric dentist with about 6 years of clinical experience and a graduate of the Altınbaş University Doctorate Programme in Paediatric Dentistry.

With about 6 years of clinical experience in paediatric dentistry, Dr. Vahide Ceyda Acar focuses on protecting children's oral and dental health and carrying out treatment in a way that suits their needs.

With experience gained in various private clinics and university clinics, she works in routine paediatric procedures as well as digital anaesthesia, paediatric crowns, ICON and SDF treatments. She can also take part in treating child patients under general anaesthesia when treatment in the clinic is not suitable.

She values patient comfort through strong communication with child patients and their families, an ethical approach and multidisciplinary teamwork.

  • Digital anaesthesia
