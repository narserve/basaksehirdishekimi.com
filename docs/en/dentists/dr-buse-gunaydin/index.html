Dr. Buse Günaydın

Dentist

A graduate of İstanbul Galata University; works in orthodontics, restorative treatments and endodontics.

Dr. Buse Günaydın graduated from the Faculty of Dentistry at İstanbul Galata University. She is a dentist who aims to keep developing her professional knowledge and clinical experience, and who puts patient satisfaction and ethical working principles first.

During her university education and clinical placements she gained experience in restorative treatments, endodontics, extractions, periodontal procedures and orthodontic treatments; she has built on her professional experience through work in private clinics and continues her work in orthodontics.

Placing importance on communication with patients, Dr. Buse Günaydın aims to contribute to protecting and improving oral and dental health by taking each patient's needs into account.

Dental Clinic in Başakşehir, Kayaşehir, Istanbul
