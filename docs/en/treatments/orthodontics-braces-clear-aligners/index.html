Orthodontics: Braces and Clear Aligners in Kayaşehir

Orthodontics is the branch of dentistry that corrects misalignment of the teeth and jaws to give the patient good aesthetics, speech and chewing function. Crowded teeth affect not only appearance but also gum health, decay risk and the jaw joint.

Book an Appointment 0212 931 99 32

Who is orthodontic treatment for?

  • Patients of any age with crowded, gapped or rotated teeth
  • People with an upper and lower jaw mismatch (bite problems)
  • Early check-ups for children aged 7–10 and, if needed, jaw guidance
  • Patients whose teeth look straight but whose bite is off

Treatment options

  • Metal brackets: the most economical and effective method
  • Ceramic (tooth-coloured) brackets: less visible
  • Clear aligners: removable and almost invisible; for suitable cases
  • Removable appliances: for children who are still growing

Treatment process

  • Consultation, panoramic and cephalometric X-rays, model analysis.
  • Explanation of the treatment plan and options.
  • Fitting of brackets or aligners; check-ups every 4–8 weeks.
  • Treatment lasts from 6 months to 3 years, followed by a retention period.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an age limit for orthodontic treatment?

No. As long as your teeth and gums are healthy, orthodontics can be done at any age. For children, a first check-up at around age 7 is recommended.

Clear aligners or braces?

Clear aligners offer invisibility and comfort and are ideal for mild to moderate cases. For serious bite problems, fixed brackets give stronger results. The right choice is decided at your consultation.

Do braces hurt?

For the first 2–3 days after fitting, and after each adjustment, you feel mild pressure; this is normal and passes quickly.

Do the teeth go back once treatment is finished?

They can if a retainer or fixed wire is not used. That is why the retention period after treatment is part of the treatment.
