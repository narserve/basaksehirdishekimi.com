Dental Fillings in Kayaşehir, Istanbul

A filling replaces tooth structure lost to decay or fracture, restoring the shape and function of the tooth. Tooth-coloured composite fillings are done in a single visit and are aesthetically unnoticeable.

Book an Appointment 0212 931 99 32

Types of fillings

  • Composite (white) filling: single visit, aesthetic, bonds to the tooth
  • Inlay-onlay (porcelain) filling: a durable restoration made in the laboratory for large areas of lost tooth structure
  • Composite bonding: closing chips and gaps in front teeth

How does the treatment proceed?

  • The depth of the decay is determined with an examination and X-ray.
  • Local anaesthesia if needed; the decay is removed completely.
  • The composite is placed in layers and hardened with light.
  • Bite check and polishing. Total 30–45 minutes.

After a filling

Do not eat until the anaesthetic has worn off. Sensitivity to cold is normal in the first few days. Brush the filled tooth as usual; avoid biting hard objects.

Frequently asked questions

How long do white fillings last?

On average 7–10 years with regular care. The size and position of the filling and any clenching habit affect its lifespan.

White filling or amalgam (silver) filling?

Composite fillings are aesthetic, bond to the tooth to strengthen it and require less tooth material to be removed. That is why they are the preferred option today.

Is pain normal after a filling?

Sensitivity to hot and cold can last from a few days to a few weeks. If it hurts when you bite, the filling may be high; a quick adjustment solves it.

Can I have a filling during pregnancy?

Yes. It is done safely, especially in the second trimester; leaving a painful, infected tooth untreated is riskier.
