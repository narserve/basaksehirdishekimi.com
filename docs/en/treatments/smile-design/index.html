Smile Design in Kayaşehir, Istanbul

Smile design combines dentistry and artistry to plan the ideal smile for you. Your facial features, gender, age, lip and gum structure, and the alignment and colour of your teeth are all assessed together; the result is not artificial, but a smile that is truly yours.

Book an Appointment 0212 931 99 32

What is assessed in smile design?

  • Facial features and smile symmetry
  • Tooth shape suited to age and gender
  • Alignment, length and colour of the teeth
  • Lip line and gum visibility
  • Jaw relationship and chewing function

Which treatments are used?

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Zirconia crowns
  • Teeth whitening
  • Minor corrections with composite bonding
  • Gum contouring (pink aesthetics)
  • Preparatory orthodontics if needed

How does the treatment proceed?

  • Consultation, photographs and X-ray analysis.
  • The new smile is designed digitally or on a wax model; a temporary trial (mock-up) is available on request.
  • After your approval, minimal preparation of the teeth and the laboratory stage.
  • Permanent restorations are bonded in place and checked. About 7–14 days on average.

Frequently asked questions

How many appointments does smile design take?

Usually 2–4 appointments over a total of 1–2 weeks. It takes longer if orthodontics or gum treatment is needed.

Does smile design damage the teeth?

Very little tooth material is removed for veneers and bonding, and in some cases none at all. When properly planned, it does not harm the tooth.

How long do the results last?

Porcelain veneers and zirconia restorations last 10–15 years or more with good care. The effect of whitening lasts 1–3 years.

Can I see the result beforehand?

Yes. With a digital design and an in-mouth mock-up you can try your new smile before any permanent work begins.
